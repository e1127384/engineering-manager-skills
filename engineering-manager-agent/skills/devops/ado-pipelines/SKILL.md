# Azure DevOps Pipelines

Identify the exact project, pipeline, run, branch, and artifact. Read status and
logs to establish what happened before recommending action. Confirm that
parameters and target environments are expected before starting or retrying a
run, and follow required approvals. Verify the resulting artifact and deployment
status; do not expose secrets from logs.

# Release Readiness

Check that scope is understood; required tests and reviews pass; known defects
are accepted by the proper owner; security, data, and operational concerns are
addressed; monitoring and support are prepared; and deployment and recovery
steps are documented. Record evidence, exceptions, approvers, and the go/no-go
decision. Escalate unmet mandatory gates rather than waiving them silently.

# Sprint Planning

- Confirm the sprint goal and available team capacity, including known absences.
- Select ready work based on priority, dependencies, and historical throughput.
- Check that selected items have owners, acceptance criteria, and appropriate
  sizing or uncertainty notes.
- Identify cross-team dependencies and risks before confirming the plan.
- Communicate the plan as a forecast, then track changes transparently.
